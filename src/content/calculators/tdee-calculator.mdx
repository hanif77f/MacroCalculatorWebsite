## What is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure is the number of calories you burn in a
day including activity — your maintenance calorie level.

## How it's calculated

BMR (via your chosen formula) × activity multiplier = TDEE. Eat below
TDEE to lose weight, above to gain, at TDEE to maintain.

## FAQ

**How is TDEE different from BMR?**
BMR is calories at complete rest. TDEE adds activity on top of that.
