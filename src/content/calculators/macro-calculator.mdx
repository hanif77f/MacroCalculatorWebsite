## What is a macro calculator?

A macro calculator estimates your daily calorie need, then splits it into
grams of protein, carbohydrate, and fat based on your goal.

## How we calculate macros

We estimate BMR using Mifflin-St Jeor by default (or Katch-McArdle /
Cunningham if you provide body fat %), scale it to TDEE with your
activity level, adjust for your goal, then split the result into macros.

## FAQ

**How accurate is a macro calculator?**
Typically within 10–15% of true maintenance. Adjust based on real-world
results over 2–3 weeks.
